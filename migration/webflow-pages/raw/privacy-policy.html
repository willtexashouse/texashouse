Privacy Policy
Effective Date: May 12, 2025

Texas House (“us”, “we”, or “our”) operates the https://texashouse.org website and associated services (collectively, the “Service”). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

We use your data to provide and improve the Service, including event participation, partnership communications, and digital engagement. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Unless otherwise defined in this Privacy Policy, terms used have the same meanings as in our Terms and Conditions, accessible from https://texashouse.global.

Information Collection and Use

We collect several types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service.

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data
While using our Service (e.g. RSVPs, newsletters, contact forms), we may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information (“Personal Data”) that can be used to contact or identify you. This may include, but is not limited to:

  • Email address
  • First and last name
  • Phone number
  • Company/Organization name
  • Job title
  • Postal address (if applicable)
  • Cookies and usage data

Usage Data
We may also collect information on how the Service is accessed and used. This “Usage Data” may include details such as:

  • Your device’s Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited
  • Time and date of visit
  • Time spent on pages
  • Unique device identifiers
  • Diagnostic data

Tracking & Cookies Data

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to monitor activity on our Service. Cookies are small files that may include an anonymous unique identifier, stored on your device. Tracking technologies may include beacons, tags, and scripts to analyze and improve our Service.

You can configure your browser to refuse all cookies or indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, disabling cookies may affect some features of the Service.

Examples of Cookies used:

  • Session Cookies: to operate our Service
  • Preference Cookies: to remember your settings and preferences
  • Security Cookies: to ensure data integrity and safety

Use of Data

Texas House uses collected data to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Notify you of changes or updates
  • Enable participation in events, programs, or surveys
  • Communicate with you regarding partnerships, opportunities, and announcements
  • Provide customer care and support
  • Improve and personalize your experience
  • Monitor Service usage and detect technical issues
  • Ensure compliance with applicable laws

Legal Basis for Processing Personal Data (under GDPR)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for collecting and using personal information depends on the data and context. We may process your data:

Text Messaging Terms

Effective Date: May 13, 2025

By providing your mobile phone number to Texas House and affirmatively opting in, you consent to receive SMS or MMS messages from Texas House regarding events, community updates, important announcements, and other relevant communications. No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. This excludes text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Message Frequency

You may receive up to 4 text messages per month. Message frequency may vary based on program activity or user engagement.

Opting Out

You may opt out at any time by texting STOP to (206) 761-7316. Upon doing so, you will receive a confirmation message and will no longer receive texts from us unless you opt in again.
