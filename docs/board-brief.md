### The company today
Texas House operates through Horizon Discovery Group, LLC, a Texas company wholly owned by Will Herrmann. It is being converted to a Texas corporation, taxed as a C-corp, with equity issued to the founding team and the board. The board is seated at that conversion.

## Purpose of the board
The board of a private, for-profit corporation. Directors owe their duty to the shareholders, hold equity, and are elected each year by the controlling shareholder. Three jobs: **Raise** (sponsors, partners, capital — every seat maps to money or legitimacy that leads to money), **Govern** (hold the company to a standard; ask the hard questions before a partner or investor does), **Recruit** (reach further than the founding team can). Equity is the engine; the vesting cliff and annual election tie reward to work.

## How the board works
Shareholders elect directors annually; Will holds control. Board of Directors: five seats, one-year terms, fiduciary, votes; Governance & Nominating Committee. Advisory Board alongside: no vote, no liability, network and doors — can form immediately, does not wait for conversion. Officers (CEO, Secretary, Treasurer) appointed by the board; attend and present, do not vote unless they hold a seat.

- Five seats, odd number, no ties; chairman holds the tiebreak until seat five is filled. Three independents alongside the founder.
- Quarterly meetings; written consents for routine approvals between.
- One committee to start (governance and nominating). Finance/audit follows when the numbers warrant it.
- Annual accountability: each director gets a plain written expectation (introductions, partners, capital, recruits), reviewed by the committee before the annual election. Advisors carry a shorter version.

- **Advisory board: Network and Doors** — one specific network or door (capital, defense, state government, music, media). Advisor agreement, same cliff, no vote, no D&O exposure.

## Terms for directors
- **Term:** one year; elected annually; re-election follows the committee's annual review.
- **Compensation:** restricted common stock at seating; no cash; expenses reimbursed. Grant size per seat, informed by the governance chair and early-stage practice.
- **Vesting:** 12-month cliff, then monthly over 36 months. Leave before the cliff → no shares. 83(b) within 30 days of grant; company provides form and deadline.
- **Expectations:** four quarterly meetings (in person where possible); consents within five business days; a written year-one expectation tied to the seat spec; conflicts disclosed, recusal where appropriate.
- **Protection:** D&O in place before the first meeting; indemnification in the bylaws; a director agreement (term, grant, expectations, exit).
- **Why the cliff, not probation:** same job, the structure experienced directors expect; waiting to grant makes the stock a larger taxable event; a grant on paper commits in a way a promise does not.

## How we fill the open seats
Specification first · committee owns it · three names from each director · warm approach, one conversation with Will · written offer before anyone says yes · committee recommends, board approves, shareholders elect. **Not doing:** filling a seat to fill it; recruiting from the founder's immediate circle; promising equity before spec, expectation, and paperwork are set.
