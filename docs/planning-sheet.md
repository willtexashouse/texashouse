## CMS notes (from handoff)

We will likely need multiple CMS types:

- **Upcoming events** — hosted on our Luma, but styled to our brand on the
  website while linking out to the Luma.
- **Event Schedule** — only present when we have an event about to take place.
  Shows our schedule, sponsors, and usually a landscape graphic of the panel we
  will be hosting. Below this, a full list of event sponsors:
    - Partners
    - Food & Beverage Partners
    - Experience Partners
  (Examples to follow.)
- **Newsroom** — basically a blog. Requires a featured article, a working
  filter by topic, and an archive.

Open items:
- CMS items will be exported from the existing website for review and import.
- Examples of the event schedule / sponsor tiers to follow.
