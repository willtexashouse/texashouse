PROMPT 1 — volume, the full field

A Texas Hill Country bluebonnet meadow at full bloom seen from a distance, drawn as a printed crest landscape. Thousands of bluebonnet stalks pack the field edge to edge with almost no open ground: the nearest row about a fifth of the frame tall, each stalk a clean flat shape, bluebonnet-blue body, cream tip, sage leaves, one ivory outline; every row behind smaller and denser, outlines dropping to ivory-dim #e7decd and blue darkening, until the far field is a solid texture of blue dots with cream tips against the ridge. Four flat live oaks in dark sage along the ridge at uneven spacing. One clay accent: a loose scatter of Indian paintbrush stalks through the near rows. No text, no logos, no photorealism, no cartoon.

PROMPT 3 — volume, elevated

A bluebonnet meadow at full bloom seen from a low rise looking down across it, drawn as a printed crest landscape, so the field reads as one deep blue texture from the bottom edge to the ridge: tens of thousands of small bluebonnet stalks, the nearest a sixth of the frame tall with bluebonnet-blue bodies, cream tips, sage leaves and ivory outlines, compressing within a few rows into a dense pattern of blue dots with ivory-dim #e7decd tips, broken only by two faint cattle trails in ivory-dim that curve out of the frame on either side.
