FOOTER BLUEBONNETS — prompt set (2026-09-04)

Direction from Will: a distant view with volume. Many stalks, rows receding,
built to be extended sideways, because the footer band is roughly five to one
on large screens and no generator renders that wide in one pass.

Reference to attach every time: public/brand/emblem-brand-hero-globe-1x1-v1.png
(the approved hero globe). Midjourney: upload it and use it as --sref, or as an
image prompt if --sref is unavailable. Higgsfield: attach as image_references.

Parameters. Midjourney: --ar 21:9 --style raw --stylize 120 (spec: 100 to 250).
Higgsfield: nano_banana_pro, 2k, 21:9. Use the widest ratio the tool allows.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OPENING (paste in front of every prompt)

Draw in exactly the reference emblem's language and nothing else: flat two-tone shapes, one even ivory #faf6f0 ink outline on every form, no interior detail, no gradients, no cast light, halftone dot pattern and paper grain over flat fills. Do not include the globe or the hat. Palette is tokens only: ivory #faf6f0, gun metal #181210, bluebonnet #3d4a76, sage #6b7a5a, clay #793f34 as the single warm accent.

This is a website footer band. The whole composition sits in the bottom 45 percent of the frame and runs edge to edge; the top 55 percent is flat empty gun metal with nothing in it. No sky, no clouds, no sun, no horizon glow. The scene continues past both side edges: no object is cut by the frame and nothing is centered, so the image can be extended left and right without a seam.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PROMPT A — the distant meadow (run this first)

A Texas Hill Country bluebonnet meadow seen from a distance, drawn as a printed crest landscape. Hundreds of bluebonnet stalks in loose receding rows fill the field from edge to edge: the nearest row about a tenth of the frame height, each stalk a clean flat shape, bluebonnet-blue body, cream tip, sage leaves, ivory outline; the rows behind smaller and closer together, their outline dropping to ivory-dim #e7decd and their blue darker, until the farthest row is a texture of dots against a low ridge line drawn once in ivory-dim. Three or four flat live oak silhouettes in dark sage stand along the ridge at uneven spacing. One clay accent only: a scattering of five or six Indian paintbrush stalks among the near rows. Open gun metal shows between the near stalks and above the ridge. No text, no logos, no photorealism, no soft shading, no cartoon.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PROMPT B — the distant meadow with the two-track

The same distant bluebonnet meadow, drawn as a printed crest landscape, with a clay dirt two-track cutting across the field on a long diagonal from the lower left toward the right, low in the frame, that exits the frame on both sides. Hundreds of bluebonnet stalks in loose receding rows on both sides of the track: nearest row about a tenth of the frame height, bluebonnet-blue bodies, cream tips, sage leaves, ivory outline; rows behind smaller, closer, ivory-dim outline, darker blue, ending in a texture of dots against a single ridge line in ivory-dim with a few flat live oak silhouettes in dark sage. The track is the only clay in the picture. Open gun metal between the near stalks and above the ridge. No text, no logos, no photorealism, no soft shading, no cartoon.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
EXTENDING TO FOOTER WIDTH (the edit step)

Target: about 5:1, with the scene never cut at a screen edge.

Midjourney: Pan left and Pan right on the chosen render, once each, same
prompt. Pan keeps the drawing and adds a frame's width per side. Zoom Out
does not help here: it shrinks the field and adds sky.

Higgsfield: outpaint_image on the chosen render, extending left and right,
same opening text and the render attached. One pass.

Either way, then crop to the bottom 45 percent and file with
  npm run hf -- intake <file> --tier emblem --register land --subject footer-bluebonnets --ar 5:1

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYER SEPARATION (after the master is approved)

Each run attaches the previous result as the reference and opens with:
"Reproduce the reference exactly, same drawing, same grain, with one change
only."

1. "Remove every stalk in the nearest two rows, filling the space with flat
   gun metal. Everything else identical."           → mid+far plate
2. On that result: "Remove all remaining flowers, leaving only the ridge
   line, the oaks, and the track."                    → far plate

Layers are cut as the difference between versions (master minus mid+far =
near; mid+far minus far = mid), so every layer is the same drawing. The
BluebonnetField component takes them as far / mid / front.
